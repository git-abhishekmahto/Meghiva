Hostel Rules

Some Strict Rules of Hostel

  • If any stüdent is found in any other room after 10 pm, then after 3 notices, a provision has been made to directly expel him/her from the hostel without returning the security amount.
  • If any student is found abusing, threatening or assaulting anyone, he/she will be immediately expelled from the hostel without returning the security amount.
  • If any student is found consuming any kind of intoxicant, legal action will be taken against him and he will be expelled from the hostel without returning the security amount.
  • If any student is found intentionally dirtying or blocking the basin and sink, a fine of Rs 500 will be imposed on the spot.
  • If any student turns on the motor without asking, he will have to pay a fine of Rs 500.
  • If any student keeps any weapon or any illegal item in his room in an unofficial manner, legal action will be taken against him and he will be arrested along with being expelled from the hostel.
  • Rooms must be kept clean and organized at all times. Random inspections will be conducted.
  • On the basis of indiscipline and negligence in duty, any student will be immediately expelled from the hostel without returning the security amount.
  • Any student must switch off the power of his room before leaving the room, otherwise a fine of Rs. 150 per day will be charged if caught.
  • Any damage to hostel property must be reported immediately, and repair costs will be charged to the student.
